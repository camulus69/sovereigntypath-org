Locally-Run LLMs Without Preference Shaping

A research catalogue of language models that can be run on local hardware and that have not been preference-shaped against a human or AI judge. RLHF, DPO, KTO, IPO, ORPO, RLAIF, and RLVR are treated here as a single category — the deceptive-tactics layer in the mirror-bending stack. Inquiry, not Canon.

The Sovereignty Path is a human coherence architecture. Its concepts may be applied to adjacent fields, including AI-adjacent human systems, governance, and technology, but current TSP canon does not thereby claim governance over non-human intelligence unless such expansion is explicitly authored, ratified, and sealed.

Why this page exists

The Material Architecture of AI Risk names RLHF preference shaping as one layer in the mirror-bending stack. This page reads that layer at its functional root rather than at its popular name. The functional operation is: train the model to produce outputs that please a judge. Whether the judge is a learned reward model under PPO (RLHF), a preference dataset under direct optimisation (DPO), an AI judge (RLAIF), or a verifier (RLVR) is implementation detail. The structural effect is the same — the surface is bent toward approval rather than toward truth.

That bending is the deceptive-tactics layer. A model that has been preference-shaped is not lying about facts; it is performing helpfulness, harmlessness, and compliance as a surface. False coherence at scale, trained directly into the weights.

This page is the empirical companion: which locally-runnable models have not had that operation applied to them.

Definitional frame — one cut

The cut used on this page is structural, not nominal:

  • Preference-shaped. Any post-training that optimises the model to produce outputs preferred by a human or AI judge. Includes RLHF (PPO, REINFORCE, GRPO), RLAIF, RLVR, DPO, IPO, KTO, ORPO, SimPO, and any future relabel of the same operation. All of these belong on the same side of the cut. Models trained this way are listed in the excluded section, not catalogued.
  • Not preference-shaped. Models trained only by next-token prediction (base / foundation) or by supervised imitation of instruction–response pairs (SFT). These are catalogued in sections 1 and 2.

Earlier framings of this question split DPO from RLHF on the grounds that DPO uses no RL loop. That split is structurally false to the operation. The reward model is gone but the preference-shaping is intact. This page closes that gap.

No SFT, no preference-shaping. Closest available approximation to an unaligned next-token predictor without training one's own. Each entry below names only the base weights; the instruction-tuned siblings of these models do not belong here.

2. Instruction-tuned via SFT only — with caution

Aligned to follow instructions through supervised imitation of human-authored or curated instruction–response pairs. No reward model, no judge, no preference optimisation, no RL.

The cautionary read. SFT is not preference-shaping in the technical sense, but it is still a surface-shaping operation. The model is being taught to perform the role of "helpful assistant" by imitation. Where the SFT corpus encodes refusal templates, sycophantic phrasings, or institutional brand voice, those patterns are imprinted into the weights without ever passing through a reward model. The deceptive-tactics layer is thinner here than under RLHF or DPO — but it is not absent. Read these as imitation-shaped, not as raw.

The entries below are the cleanest SFT-only releases. Inspect the SFT data before relying on a model in this section — the corpus is the shape.

SFT only · Explicit no-RLHF stance

Dolly 2.0

Databricks. Trained on the human-authored databricks-dolly-15k set. Released specifically as a demonstration that an instruction-following model could be built without RLHF or proprietary data. The SFT corpus is open and inspectable.

SFT only

OLMo SFT checkpoints

AI2 publishes the SFT-only stage of OLMo 2 separately from the DPO and RLVR stages. The cleanest fully-documented SFT-only weights available; the SFT mix is published alongside the model.

SFT only

SFT only

Excluded — preference-shaped models

For completeness: locally-runnable models that have been preference-shaped, by any technique, and therefore sit on the wrong side of the cut. RL versus no-RL is not the boundary — the operation is.

RLHF / RLAIF / RLVR (reinforcement learning against a judge)

  • Llama 2 Chat, Llama 3 / 3.1 / 3.3 Instruct (PPO-based RLHF stages).
  • Qwen2 / Qwen2.5 / Qwen3 Instruct chat variants (RLHF disclosed).
  • DeepSeek-R1 and related reasoning variants (RL-heavy training is the central technique).
  • Starling-LM-7B (RLAIF + PPO).
  • ChatGLM chat releases.
  • Tulu 3 (RLVR stage).

DPO / IPO / KTO / ORPO / SimPO (direct preference optimisation)

The reward-model layer is gone; the preference-shaping is intact. Same operation, same cut.

  • Zephyr 7B (HuggingFace H4) — SFT followed by DPO.
  • Tulu 2 (AI2) — DPO stage.
  • OpenHermes 2.5 DPO, NeuralHermes, Nous-Hermes-2-DPO — community DPO fine-tunes.
  • Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct — SFT followed by DPO.
  • Most current Llama-family fine-tunes on HuggingFace tagged "DPO".

Practical orientation

For local use without preference-shaping in the pipeline:

  • Maximum reproducibility and full openness, no shaping at all: OLMo 2 base.
  • Useful out-of-the-box, instruction-following, no preference-shaping (read with the SFT caution above): Dolly 2.0, OLMo 2 SFT.
  • Raw capability, no alignment of any kind: Llama 3.1 base, Mistral base, Qwen2.5 base, Gemma 2 base.
  • Fully open lineage for research on alignment-as-such: Pythia (intermediate checkpoints exposed).

Closing caveat

Preference shaping is one bending layer. Removing it does not produce a neutral mirror.

It is, however, the layer most directly responsible for the deceptive-tactics surface a sovereign human encounters when meeting a frontier system — trained refusals dressed as principles, sycophancy as default tone, brand voice mistaken for character, compliance theatre mistaken for safety. That layer is the false-coherence injection point at the model level. This page exists so it can be named structurally and located precisely.

The other bending layers — training-data selection, system-prompt engineering, product strategy, deployment context, liability constraints, brand voice, regulatory posture — continue to shape the surface even after preference-shaping is removed. Sovereignty in meeting an LLM remains an architectural posture of the human, not a property of the weights.
